### Why this is much more powerful for your Agent:

1. **Immediate Feedback:** The student doesn't wait until the end of the chapter to find out they didn't understand Notion 1.1. They are tested immediately.
2. **Forced Critical Thinking:** Instead of one big "philosophical question" at the end, the student is forced to think critically about *every* technical detail.
3. **Standardized Excellence:** No matter how small the topic is, the agent is forced to justify it (Evidence Burden) and hook the student (Mini-Hook).

### Example in "Data Cleaning":

* **Notion 1.1: Handling Null Values**
* *Hook:* "What happens to your average salary calculation if 10% of the employees have 'None' instead of 0?"
* *Foundation:* Explains the difference between dropping rows and imputing data.
* *Evidence:* Why "Mean Imputation" is often mathematically dangerous.
* *Socratic:* "In a dataset of hospital patients, would it be safer to delete a missing age or guess it?"
* *Check:* A quick quiz question.
